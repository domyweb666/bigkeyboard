## 第二步：照這個順序錄（大約 3 分鐘）

用控制中心的「螢幕錄影」。從主畫面開始錄，Apple 要求一定要從打開 App 開始。

1. 在主畫面點豆鍵圖示，打開後停在「字典」分頁兩秒
2. 切到「使用說明」分頁，停兩秒，讓「加入鍵盤」那段入鏡
3. 在同一頁按「前往設定」> 點「鍵盤」> 打開「豆鍵」> 打開「允許完全取用」> 按「允許」（已經開著就略過）。設定頁看不到開關，就走 設定 > 一般 > 鍵盤 > 鍵盤 > 新增鍵盤 > 豆鍵，再回來開完全取用
4. 打開備忘錄，新增一則，按住地球鍵選豆鍵
5. 打 ㄋㄧˇㄏㄠˇ，點候選列的「你好」
6. 再打一句平常會打的話，打的時候按住一顆鍵，讓放大泡泡入鏡
7. 打 ㄧˋ，按候選列右邊的 ⌄ 展開全部候選，再按一次收起
8. 回豆鍵 App 的「字典」分頁，按右下角＋，輸入「豆鍵」> 按「加入」，看到它出現後左滑刪掉
9. 切到「主題」分頁，點「糖豆」，看到打勾
10. 回備忘錄，按候選列最右邊的鍵盤圖示收起鍵盤，再點一下文字區叫出來，讓糖豆配色入鏡，打幾個字
11. 回「主題」分頁，捲到最下面按「回復購買」，等「已回復 7 款主題」的提示出現。如果跳出 Apple 帳號登入就照著登入
12. 停止錄影，從頭看一次，確認沒有通知或個資入鏡

字典頁的黃色提醒卡這次不會出現，因為這支手機的鍵盤已經寫過資料，所以從「使用說明」分頁進設定。

如果選了主題鍵盤沒換，或回復購買沒有顯示「已回復 7 款主題」，先停下來把畫面傳給我，不要送審。

不要為了重現購買視窗去刪 App 重裝：字典會清空，而且買過的紀錄還在，一樣跳不出購買視窗。

錄影裡的文字不要有別人的對話或個資。

路徑：豆鍵 > App Store 分頁 > iOS 1.0 > 往下到「App 審查資訊」。

1. 錄影傳到「附件」
2. 下面整段貼到「備註」，把原本的備註蓋掉（舊內容都併進來了）
3. 儲存，build 不換，重新送審

如果頁面上沒有附件欄，把影片傳到 YouTube 設「不公開」，把備註第 1 段第一句換成：`Screen recording (no login needed): 【影片網址】`

```
1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached in App Review Information. It starts by launching the app and shows adding the keyboard, turning on Full Access, typing, the dictionary, selecting a paid theme, using it in the keyboard, and Restore Purchases. The Apple Account in the recording already bought all 7 paid themes in TestFlight, so the themes show as owned and the purchase sheet does not appear in the recording. A new account sees the price on each paid theme and gets the Apple purchase sheet when tapping it.
The app has no accounts, no login, and no user-generated content shared with other people, so there are no registration, deletion, reporting, or blocking flows.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
This is a Zhuyin (Bopomofo) keyboard for typing Traditional Chinese, for people in Taiwan who type Zhuyin on iPhone and often hit the wrong key. The Zhuyin layout has many small keys, so a neighboring key is easy to hit by mistake.
The keyboard shows larger symbols on the keys and a popup of the key being pressed. It colors the medial and final symbols so they are easy to tell apart, enlarges the touch area of likely next symbols, and treats a tap between keys as the nearest key.
The companion app has three tabs: dictionary (字典), themes (主題), and guide (使用說明).

3. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
No login or sample files are needed.
- Add the keyboard: Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > 豆鍵.
- Switch to it: in any text field, tap the globe key.
- Try typing: tap ㄋ ㄧ ˇ ㄏ ㄠ ˇ, then tap 你好 in the candidate bar.
- Full Access is optional. To turn it on: Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > 豆鍵 > Allow Full Access. It is used only to let the keyboard and the app share the user dictionary and theme setting through an App Group. The keyboard contains no networking code and collects no data. Without Full Access, typing works normally, but the keyboard does not learn words and uses the free default theme.
- Dictionary: open the 字典 tab. Words the keyboard has learned appear here. Tap + to add a word, swipe left to delete one.
- Themes: open the 主題 tab. 冷灰 is free. Tap a theme showing a price to open the Apple purchase sheet. After purchase it is selected automatically. Dismiss and reopen the keyboard to see it. Full Access must be on for the keyboard to show a paid theme. 回復購買 at the bottom of the tab restores purchases.
The 7 paid themes are non-consumable In-App Purchases included in this submission.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
The only external service is Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). There are no servers of our own, and no analytics, ads, AI services, authentication services, or third-party payment processors.
Open-source components are bundled and run on the device: McBopomofo input engine and word data (MIT), Noto Serif TC font and LXGW WenKai TC font (SIL Open Font License).

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions the same in all regions. There is no region-specific code or content. The interface is in Traditional Chinese. In-App Purchase prices come from Apple for each storefront.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry and has no protected third-party material. The open-source components above are used under their licenses, and the license files are bundled with the app.
```
