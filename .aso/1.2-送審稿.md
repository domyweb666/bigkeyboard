## 一、新功能說明（What's New）

### 繁體中文

```
這一版多了幾個改錯字、打符號比較省事的做法，也修了英文大寫的問題。

按住空白鍵可以移游標。按住半秒，鍵上的字淡掉之後左右拖，游標跟著走。

按錯了先別放開，滑到對的那顆鍵再放開，打出來的是放開時那顆。

按住「符」往上滑，符號頁會先跑出來。滑到要的符號再放開，打完還在注音鍵盤，不用自己切回來。

按住刪除鍵久一點，會從一次刪一個字變成一次刪好幾個字，碰到標點會停一下。

在 email 和網址的輸入框，鍵盤會自己換成英文，多一顆 @ 或 .com。

設定頁多了「按鍵震動」和「按鍵聲」。按鍵聲可以選三套機械鍵盤的聲音，不想要聲音也可以關掉。這兩個要打開「允許完全取用」才有作用，不開也能照常打字。

修正：之前在空的輸入框切到英文，⇧ 會一直亮著、按了也關不掉。現在英文不會自動大寫，要大寫自己按 ⇧，點一下大寫一個字，連點兩下鎖定大寫。
```

### 英文（英國）

```
This version adds a few quicker ways to fix mistakes and type symbols, and fixes a Shift problem on the English keyboard.

Hold the space bar to move the cursor. After half a second the key labels fade; drag left or right and the cursor follows.

Pressed the wrong key? Keep your finger down, slide to the one you wanted and let go. The key you let go on is the one that gets typed.

Hold the symbols key and slide up. The symbols page appears; let go on a symbol and you are back on the Zhuyin keyboard.

Hold delete a little longer and it goes from one character at a time to several, pausing at punctuation.

In email and web address fields the keyboard switches to English, with an extra @ or .com key.

Settings has two new options: key haptics and key sounds. Key sounds include three mechanical keyboard sounds, and you can turn sounds off. Both need Allow Full Access; typing works the same without it.

Fixed: in an empty text field, ⇧ on the English keyboard used to stay on and wouldn't turn off. English no longer capitalises on its own. Tap ⇧ for one capital letter, or double-tap it to lock capitals.
```

App 裡的「新功能」卡片這次會跳（1.1 → 1.2），內容在 `App/Veggie/WhatsNew.swift`，三點：移游標和刪除加速、滑著打、震動和按鍵聲。

## 二、App 審查資訊的備註（英文）

1.1 的備註原樣留著，最前面加這段：

```
New in 1.2 (in the keyboard extension unless noted):
- Hold the space bar for about 0.4 s, then drag to move the cursor (UITextDocumentProxy.adjustTextPosition).
- Slide from one key to another before releasing. Hold the symbols key and slide to a symbol. Hold Shift and slide to a letter.
- Holding delete speeds up after about 2 s.
- Email and URL fields show the English layout with an @ key, or / and .com keys.
- App > Settings: a key haptics switch and a key sound menu, including three CC0 mechanical keyboard sounds (credits under open source licences). Haptics are off and sound is the system default until the user changes them. Both need Allow Full Access because the keyboard reads the choice from the App Group container. Full Access stays optional. The keyboard makes no network requests.
- Fixed: Shift on the English layout stayed on in an empty text field. English auto-capitalisation has been removed.
No new in-app purchases. Privacy answers are unchanged.
```

## 三、送審前要知道的

- 這一版的新功能只在雲端模擬器上驗過行為，沒有在真機測過手感、震動和聲音（紀錄在 `.vibe/總測試清單.md` 最後一段）。多米 2026-11-16 決定一起送。
- 「落點往上修正」會影響每一下按鍵。上線後如果覺得變得常按到上一排，把 `SamsungTheme.touchLiftPoints` 改成 0 再出一版就能關掉。
- 描述、關鍵字、截圖都沿用 1.1，這次沒改。描述裡沒有跟新功能衝突的句子（對過「鍵盤沒有任何連網功能」「豆鍵沒有的」兩段）。
- 隱私：沒有新的連網、權限或資料去向。震動和按鍵聲的設定存在 App 和鍵盤共用的資料夾，不離開手機。隱私標籤維持「不收集資料」。
- 加速審核：這版不是純修 bug，照規矩不一定收。線上的 ⇧ 問題如果很急，可以申請，理由寫那個 bug。
