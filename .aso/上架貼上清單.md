### 描述（1295／4000）

```
豆鍵是字大的注音鍵盤，排法跟你習慣的一樣，打的字只留在你的手機上。

我換到 iPhone 之後，打注音一直按到旁邊的鍵，一句話要刪好幾次。原生鍵盤的注音字細、鍵又擠，我手指不細，乾脆自己做一個。

豆鍵就是這樣來的。一排豆子，一顆豆一個鍵。

【打起來哪裡不一樣】
- 鍵帽上的注音字比較大，聲調符號另外放大加粗，不會細到只剩一個點
- 第二、三排左右錯開半顆，鍵跟第一排一樣寬，不會越排越窄
- 介音剛好排成中間一直欄，塗成淡藍；韻母塗成淡橘。聲母在左、韻母在右，一眼分得出來
- 按下去會跳出放大的字，看得到自己按了哪顆
- 打了聲母之後，介音和韻母的感應範圍會悄悄變大，鍵的外觀不變
- 手指按在兩顆鍵中間的縫，算離得最近的那顆，不會沒反應

【選字】
- 注音引擎和詞庫用開源的小麥注音，台灣用語、常用詞都有
- 聲調可以不打，豆鍵會自己找
- 空白鍵可以當一聲：沒打聲調就按空白，候選只剩一聲的字，再按一下送出。空白鍵底下的小字會寫「一聲」或「選字」，看得出這一下會做什麼。不習慣的話，設定頁可以改回直接選字
- 你改選過的詞會記下來，下次排在前面
- 鍵盤會看游標前面一兩個字，把你平常接在後面打的詞排到前面
- 候選字太多，按候選列右邊的展開鍵，一次看完

【字典】
打開豆鍵 App 就看得到鍵盤自動學到的詞。人名、店名、產品名這種詞庫沒有的，可以自己加，注音會自動帶出來。不要的詞左滑刪掉。字典可以匯出成檔案備份，換手機時再匯入。

【剪貼簿】
注音鍵盤上面那排有一顆剪貼簿鍵，按一下，剛複製的字就收進來，點一下貼上。常用的可以固定、加標籤，到豆鍵 App 的剪貼簿分頁改字。

【主題】
預設的冷灰免費。另外有 7 款付費主題，買一次就一直能用：毛豆、豆漿、紅豆、黑豆、咖啡豆、蝶豆花、糖豆。主題換的是鍵帽顏色、字體和圓角，鍵位和手感都不變。每款都有淺色、深色兩套，跟著手機的深色模式切換。豆漿用明體鍵帽字，紅豆用楷書。

【隱私】
鍵盤沒有任何連網功能。你打的字、鍵盤學到的詞、剪貼簿，都存在你自己的手機上，我看不到。

設定頁有一個 iCloud 同步，預設是關的。打開之後，字典裡的詞會存到你自己的 iCloud；剪貼簿和打字紀錄不會上傳。

設定裡要開「允許完全取用」，是因為 iOS 規定鍵盤要把資料寫進 App 的共用資料夾，就得開這個。不開也能照常打字，只是鍵盤不會學詞、剪貼簿不能用，主題也會固定用冷灰。

【開始使用】
第一次打開豆鍵 App，會帶你去設定把豆鍵加進鍵盤，再讓你試打一個字。也可以自己到「設定 > 一般 > 鍵盤 > 鍵盤 > 新增鍵盤」，選豆鍵。打字時按地球鍵切換鍵盤；要打表情符號，按住地球鍵切回系統鍵盤。

【豆鍵沒有的】
表情符號頁、語音輸入、英文自動更正，這些都沒做。我只想把打注音這件事做好。

注音引擎與詞庫：小麥注音（McBopomofo，MIT 授權）
豆漿主題字體：Noto Serif TC（SIL Open Font License）
紅豆主題字體：霞鶩文楷 TC（SIL Open Font License）
```

### 描述（3620／4000）

```
Doujian is a Zhuyin (Bopomofo) keyboard with bigger letters on the keys. The layout is the standard one you already know, and what you type stays on your phone.

When I moved to iPhone, I kept hitting the key next to the one I wanted. The Zhuyin letters on the built-in keyboard are thin and the keys are tight, and my fingers aren't thin. So I built my own. Doujian means "bean keys": a row of beans, one bean per key.

WHAT FEELS DIFFERENT
- Bigger Zhuyin letters on every key. Tone marks are enlarged and bold, so they don't shrink to a dot
- Rows two and three are offset by half a key, so every key keeps the same width
- Colour zones: the medials line up in one column in light blue, the finals are light orange. Initials on the left, finals on the right
- A bubble pops up so you can see which key you pressed
- After you type an initial, the medials and finals get a slightly larger touch area. The keys look the same
- Tap the gap between two keys and it counts as the nearest one

PICKING CHARACTERS
- The engine and dictionary come from the open-source McBopomofo, with everyday Taiwanese words
- You can skip the tones and Doujian will find them
- The space bar can enter the first tone: type a syllable without a tone, press space, and only first-tone characters are left. Press space again to send. The small label on the space bar tells you which one it will do. You can switch this off in Settings
- Words you correct are remembered and come first next time
- The keyboard looks at the one or two characters before the cursor and moves the words you usually type next to the front
- Too many candidates? Tap the arrow on the right of the candidate bar to see them all

DICTIONARY
Open the Doujian app to see the words the keyboard has learned. Add names, shops and product names the dictionary doesn't have; the Zhuyin fills in by itself. Swipe left to delete. You can export the dictionary as a file and import it on a new phone.

CLIPBOARD
There's a clipboard key on the Zhuyin keyboard. Tap it and the text you just copied is saved; tap a card to paste. Pin the ones you use often, add tags, and edit them in the app.

THEMES
Cool Grey is free. Seven more themes are a one-time purchase each: Edamame, Soy Milk, Red Bean, Black Bean, Coffee Bean, Butterfly Pea and Jelly Bean. Themes change the key colours, fonts and corner shapes. The layout and feel stay the same. Each theme has a light and a dark version that follows your phone.

PRIVACY
The keyboard has no network access. What you type, the words it learns and your clipboard stay on your phone. I can't see them.

iCloud sync is in Settings and is off by default. Turn it on and your dictionary words are stored in your own iCloud. The clipboard and typing history are never uploaded.

iOS requires Full Access for a keyboard to save data to the folder it shares with its app. Without it you can still type, but the keyboard won't learn words, the clipboard won't work and the theme stays Cool Grey.

GETTING STARTED
The first time you open the Doujian app, it walks you through adding the keyboard in Settings and lets you type a test character. You can also go to Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard and choose 豆鍵. Tap the globe key to switch keyboards. For emoji, hold the globe key and switch back to the system keyboard.

WHAT DOUJIAN DOESN'T HAVE
An emoji page, voice input and English autocorrect. I just want Zhuyin typing to be good.

Zhuyin engine and dictionary: McBopomofo (MIT License)
Soy Milk theme font: Noto Serif TC (SIL Open Font License)
Red Bean theme font: LXGW WenKai TC (SIL Open Font License)
```
